FAQs

Answers Before You Book

Everything Houston homeowners and facility managers ask us most often.

Still have questions? Get a quote
Why is Elect Cleaning Services so popular?

Elect Cleaning Services is known for reputable, professional and comprehensive cleaning, warm employees, and flexibility in terms of timing as well as the quality of work delivered in homes and offices.

How frequently should you ideally engage a housekeeping service?

Frequency is entirely up to you, though most clients book weekly, bi-weekly or monthly cleaning programs to keep their place clean, hygienic and comfortable to stay in.

Should I take any extra precautions before the cleaners show up?

Securing valuables, clearing the space and giving instructions on how to access the property all help — cleaners can then work effectively and safely without any inconvenience.

If I sign up for recurring service, will the same person visit my home every time?

Yes. We make an effort to assign the same cleaning professional to recurring assignments to create familiarity, consistency and custom care of your home.

When the cleaning is being done, should I be at home?

There is no need to be home; cleaners can work alone as long as they are given access. Attending is still useful if you want to share special instructions or raise issues.

How long does the average cleaning task take?

The average cleaning period is 2–4 hours, based on the size of the property, the number of rooms and the specific services required — always with a comprehensive, detailed outcome.

What are the prices for your cleaning services?

Depending on the type of service, size of the house and frequency, prices differ. We offer transparent estimates so that every client gets fair pricing.
